About Us

The story behind M&R Paragon

Who We Are

Welcome to M&R Paragon! We are a jewellery boutique based in Gaborone, Botswana, specialising in beautiful pieces that celebrate every occasion. From everyday wear to special gifts, we have something for everyone — including a dedicated children's collection.

What We Offer

At M&R Paragon we carry carefully curated collections from some of the most recognised jewellery brands in the world, including Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels and Pandora. Every piece is chosen with elegance and inspiration in mind, because we believe jewellery is more than just an accessory — it tells a story.

Our Promise

We are committed to bringing you quality pieces that you will love and treasure. Whether you are shopping for yourself or looking for the perfect gift, M&R Paragon is here to help you find exactly what you are looking for.
